FAQs about JLCPCB PCB Assembly Capabilities
What are the minimum and maximum board sizes supported for PCBA?
Single PCB Assembly
Standard PCBA: 70 × 70 mm to 460 × 500 mm
Economic PCBA: 10 × 10 mm to 470 × 500 mm
Panelized PCB Assembly
Standard PCBA: 70 × 70 mm to 250 × 250 mm
Economic PCBA: 10 × 10 mm to 250 × 250 mm
For panelized PCBs, larger panels are more susceptible to bending and deformation during SMT processing. Therefore, we recommend keeping panel dimensions within 250 × 250 mm whenever possible.
If your panel slightly exceeds the recommended size and the design requires it, production may still be feasible.
